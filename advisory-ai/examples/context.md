# Context for AI Fitness Coach App

## Market Research

The global fitness app market is valued at $4.4 billion and growing at 14.7% CAGR. Key trends include:

- Increased demand for home fitness solutions (accelerated by COVID-19)
- Growing interest in personalized health and wellness
- Rising adoption of wearable devices and health tracking
- Shift from generic workout programs to AI-powered personalization

## User Research Insights

Based on surveys of 500 potential users:

- 73% want personalized workout plans but find current apps too generic
- 68% struggle with proper exercise form and would pay for real-time feedback
- 45% abandon fitness apps within 30 days due to lack of engagement
- 82% prefer working out at home but miss the guidance of gym trainers

## Technical Feasibility

- Computer vision for form analysis is technically challenging but achievable
- Similar technology exists in apps like Tempo Studio (hardware-based)
- Mobile processing limitations may require cloud-based ML inference
- Real-time feedback requires low-latency processing

## Competitive Analysis

**Strengths vs Competitors:**
- More personalized than Nike Training Club
- More affordable than Peloton Digital
- Better workout focus than MyFitnessPal
- Unique form-checking feature vs Fitbod

**Weaknesses:**
- No established brand like Nike or Peloton
- Higher technical complexity than existing solutions
- Requires significant ML/AI expertise
